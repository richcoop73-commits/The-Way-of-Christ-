The Way of Christ

A chaplet of strength, discipline and love

“Let all your things be done with charity.”
1 Corinthians 16:14

About this chaplet

An original Christ-centred devotion inspired by the Rosary, not a replacement for it. Seven mysteries, seven repetitions each. Allow 15–25 minutes. Prayer is not a test of masculinity or a way to earn grace. Let each virtue become an act of love. This page works without internet when opened in a browser that supports local HTML and JavaScript.
